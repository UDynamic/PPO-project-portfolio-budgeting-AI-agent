\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Domain} & \textbf{Definition} \\
\midrule
Planned start               & $s_i$                      & $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ & Scheduled period at which project $i$ begins. \\
Planned duration            & $D_i$                      & $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 1}$ & Number of active periods planned for project $i$. \\
Planned finish              & $f_i$                      & $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ & $f_i = s_i + D_i$; derived at reset. \\
S-curve shape               & $a_i,\; b_i$               & $\mathbb{R}^+$        & Beta CDF shape parameters governing $PV_i(\tau)$. \\
Progress delay cap          & $\delta^{\max}_i$          & $[0,1]$               & Maximum admissible progress lag before the breach flag fires. \\
Finish delay cap            & $\Delta^{\max}_i$          & $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$     & Extra periods beyond $f_i$ before the hard deadline fires. \\
Termination tolerance       & $\theta_i$                 & $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$     & Grace-period length; models FIDIC Sub-Clause~15.1 (FIDIC, 2017). \\
Budget at completion        & $\mathrm{BAC}_i$           & $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$     & Total planned contractor cost. \\
Profit margin               & $\pi_i$                    & $\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ & Contract price $P_i = \mathrm{BAC}_i(1+\pi_i)$. \\
Advance payment rate        & $\phi_i$                   & $[0,0.3]$             & Advance $\mathrm{Adv}_i = \phi_i P_i$ paid at $t = s_i$; $0$ disables it. \\
Advance recovery threshold  & $\psi^{\mathrm{rec}}_i$    & $[0,1]$               & Certified gross fraction above which recovery deductions start. \\
Advance recovery rate       & $\delta^{\mathrm{rec}}_i$  & $[0,1]$               & Per-milestone deduction rate used to recover $\mathrm{Adv}_i$. \\
Retention rate              & $\nu_i$                    & $[0,0.1]$             & $R^{\mathrm{held}}_{i,j} = \nu_i G_{i,j}$; released in full at $j = M_i$. \\
Milestone weight            & $w_{i,j}$                  & $[0,1]$               & Fraction of $P_i$ paid at milestone $j$; $\sum_j w_{i,j} = 1$. \\
Milestone progress threshold& $\psi_{i,j}$               & $(0,1]$               & Minimum $EV_{i,t}$ to certify milestone $j$; $\psi_{i,M_i} = 1$. \\
Milestone timestep threshold& $\tau^{\mathrm{ms}}_{i,j}$ & $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ & Earliest project time at which milestone $j$ can be certified. \\
Initial budget              & $B_0$                      & $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$     & $B_0 = \kappa \sum_i \mathrm{BAC}_i$; derived at reset. \\
Budget tightness            & $\kappa$                   & $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$     & Liquidity regime scalar. \\
Discount factor             & $\gamma$                   & $(0,1]$               & Per-period discount on net cash-flow rewards (Damodaran, 2012). \\
Annual interest rate        & $r$                        & $[0,1]$               & Monthly treasury-draw cost $= r/12$. \\
Episode horizon             & $T$                        & $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$     & Derived quantity; not a configuration entry. \\
Work efficiency             & $\eta_{i,t}$               & ${\geq}0.01$          & Sole stochastic primitive; drawn per period per active project. \\
Portfolio size              & $N$                        & $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$     & Number of projects; sampled at each reset if a distribution. \\
Milestone count             & $M_i$                      & $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$     & Number of milestones of project $i$. \\
