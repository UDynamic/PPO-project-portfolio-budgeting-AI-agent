\rowcolor{headbg}
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textcolor{headfg}{\textit{Schedule}}} \\

Planned start
  & $s_i$ & $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$
  & Scheduled project start period. \\
\rowcolor{RowAlt}
Planned duration
  & $D_i$ & $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$
  & Normalises project-time for S-curve \eqref{eq:scurve};
    $D_i=0$ collapses to a step function. \\
Planned finish
  & $f_i$ & $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$
  & $f_i = s_i + D_i$; defines the hard deadline via
    \eqref{eq:horizon} and \eqref{eq:term_cond2}. \\
S-curve shape
  & $a_i,\; b_i$ & $\mathbb{R}^+$
  & Beta CDF parameters governing $PV_i(\tau)$ \eqref{eq:scurve}. \\
\rowcolor{RowAlt}
Milestone progress threshold
  & $\psi_{i,j}$ & $(0,1]$
  & Minimum $EV_{i,t}$ to certify milestone $j$. \\
Milestone timestep threshold
  & $\tau^{\mathrm{ms}}_{i,j}$ & $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$
  & Earliest project-time for certification \citep{FIDIC2017}. \\
\rowcolor{RowAlt}
Progress delay cap
  & $\delta^{\max}_i$ & $[0,1]$
  & Maximum lag before breach flag $\mathbb{1}^{\delta}_{i,t}$
    activates \eqref{eq:term_cond1}. \\
Finish delay cap
  & $\Delta^{\max}_i$ & $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$
  & Extra periods after $f_i$ before hard deadline fires
    \eqref{eq:term_cond2}. \\
\rowcolor{RowAlt}
Termination tolerance
  & $\theta_i$ & $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$
  & Grace-period length; models FIDIC Sub-Clause~15.1
    \citep{FIDIC2017, Ndekugri2008}. \\

\rowcolor{headbg}
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textcolor{headfg}{\textit{Contract}}} \\

Budget at completion
  & $\mathrm{BAC}_i$ & $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$
  & Total planned contractor cost. \\
\rowcolor{RowAlt}
Profit margin
  & $\pi_i$ & $\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$
  & $P_i = \mathrm{BAC}_i(1+\pi_i)$. \\
Advance payment rate
  & $\phi_i$ & $[0,0.3]$
  & $\mathrm{Adv}_i=\phi_i P_i$ at $t=s_i$;
    $\phi_i=0$ disables the advance mechanism. \\
\rowcolor{RowAlt}
Advance recovery threshold
  & $\psi^{\mathrm{rec}}_i$ & $[0,1]$
  & Cumulative certified gross fraction above which
    recovery deductions commence \eqref{eq:advance_rec}. \\
Advance recovery rate
  & $\delta^{\mathrm{rec}}_i$ & $[0,1]$
  & Per-milestone deduction rate to recover
    $\mathrm{Adv}_i$ \eqref{eq:advance_rec}. \\
\rowcolor{RowAlt}
Retention rate
  & $\nu_i$ & $[0,0.1]$
  & $R^{\mathrm{held}}_{i,j}=\nu_i G_{i,j}$;
    released in full at $j=M_i$. \\
Milestone weight
  & $w_{i,j}$ & $[0,1]$
  & Fraction of $P_i$ paid at milestone $j$. \\

\rowcolor{headbg}
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textcolor{headfg}{\textit{Portfolio}}} \\

\rowcolor{RowAlt}
Initial budget
  & $B_0$ & $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$
  & $B_0=\kappa\sum_i\mathrm{BAC}_i$. \\
Budget tightness
  & $\kappa$ & $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$
  & Liquidity regime scalar. \\
\rowcolor{RowAlt}
Discount factor
  & $\gamma$ & $(0,1]$
  & Per-period discount \citep{Damodaran2012}. \\
Annual interest rate
  & $r$ & $[0,1]$
  & Monthly treasury-draw cost $=r/12$ per \eqref{eq:interest}. \\
\rowcolor{RowAlt}
Episode horizon
  & $T$ & $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$
  & Derived from \eqref{eq:horizon}. \\

\rowcolor{headbg}
\multicolumn{4}{l}{\textcolor{headfg}{\textit{Stochastic Primitive}}} \\

Work efficiency
  & $\eta_{i,t}$ & ${\geq}0.01$
  & Sole stochastic primitive; drawn per period per project
    from a configurable distribution; realised after
    $x_{i,t}$ is committed. \\
