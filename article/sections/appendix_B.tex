\section*{Conventions}
% ============================================================

All cases share these conventions unless explicitly overridden:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Each discrete timestep $t$ represents the interval from the end
    of period $t-1$ to the end of period $t$. All events assigned to
    period $t$ (spend, progress update, milestone certification, payment)
    occur at the close of that interval.
  \item $\gamma = 0.95$ throughout, imposing time value of money in
    every case.
  \item Earliest certification dates $e_{i,j}$ are set for every case.
    The default rule is $e_{i,j} = s_i +
    \lfloor(j-1)\cdot D_i^{\mathrm{plan}}/M_i\rfloor$, spreading
    milestones evenly across the planned duration. Any departure is
    stated explicitly.
  \item \textbf{Advance payment} is received at $t = s_i - 1$. For
    single-project cases where $s_i=1$, this means the advance arrives
    at $t=0$ (before the planning horizon begins). The discount factor
    for the advance term is therefore $\gamma^{s_i-1-1}=\gamma^{-1}$
    when the objective is indexed from $t=1$; equivalently, we index
    $Z$ from $t=0$ so the advance contributes $\gamma^0 \cdot A_i$.
  \item $B_1 = 3\cdot\sum_i \BAC_i$ (large; cash never binding unless
    stated).
  \item $\bar\eta_{i,t}=\bar\eta$ constant and deterministic unless
    stated.
  \item Equal milestone weights $\phi_{i,j}=1/M_i$ unless stated.
  \item $\alpha_i=0$, $\rho_i=0$ unless stated.
  \item $\delta^{\mathrm{rec}}=0.25$, $\psi=0.10$ (FIDIC defaults)
    unless stated.
  \item $\pi_i=0$ unless stated ($CP_i=\BAC_i$).
  \item S-curve shape $a_i=b_i=2$ (symmetric Beta) unless stated.
  \item Monetary units are abstract.
\end{itemize}

\begin{notebox}
\NOTEH With $\gamma=0.95$ and earliest certification dates enforced,
the optimal strategy in every completing case is to accumulate progress
so that $P_{i,t}^{\mathrm{actual}} \geq \theta_{i,j}$ is first
satisfied exactly at $t = e_{i,j}$, then certify immediately when the
window opens. Spending earlier than needed wastes discounted cash;
spending later than $e_{i,j}$ loses discount value on inflows.
\end{notebox}

\section{Group 1: Progress and Milestone Certification}
% ============================================================

\textit{What is being proven:} budget converts to progress at rate $\eta$,
milestones certify at the correct threshold and not before $e_{i,j}$,
payments trigger correctly, and milestone ordering is enforced even when
progress jumps past multiple thresholds in one period.

\subsection{SP-1: Perfect Efficiency, Two Milestones}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\paragraph{Economic claim.}
With $\eta=1$ and $\gamma=0.95$, the MILP front-loads spend so that
MS1 certifies exactly at $e_{1,1}=1$. MS2 cannot certify before
$e_{1,2}=3$ regardless of how much progress has been achieved.
Deferring the MS2 spend to $t=3$ saves discounted outflow with
identical inflow, so $Z^*=19.025>16.65$ (front-loaded).

\paragraph{Parameters.}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
$n,\,s_1,\,f_1,\,D^{\mathrm{plan}}$ & $1,\;1,\;4,\;4$\\
$\BAC_1,\;CP_1,\;\pi_1$ & $100,\;120,\;0.20$\\
$M_1,\;\theta,\;\phi$ & $2,\;(0.5,1.0),\;(0.5,0.5)$\\
$\bar\eta,\;e_{1,1},\;e_{1,2}$ & $1.0,\;1,\;3$\\
$\alpha_1,\;\rho_1,\;\Omega_1,\;\mu_1,\;\tau_1^{\mathrm{tol}}$ &
  $0,\;0,\;1,\;0.30,\;2$\\
$B_1,\;H,\;\gamma$ & $300,\;4,\;0.95$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Milestone payments.}
$R_{1,1}^{\mathrm{net}}=R_{1,2}^{\mathrm{net}}=60$.

\paragraph{Optimal allocation.}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ccccc}
\toprule
$t$ & $x_{1,t}$ & $P_{1,t}^{\mathrm{actual}}$ & Window open? & Inflow\\
\midrule

3 & 50 & 1.00 & MS2: \checkmark & 60\\
4 &  0 & 1.00 & --- & 0\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\[
  Z^* = \gamma^0(60-50)+\gamma^1(0)+\gamma^2(60-50)

\]

\subsection{SP-3: Three Milestones, \texorpdfstring{$\gamma=0.95$}{gamma=0.95}}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\paragraph{Economic claim.}
With $\gamma<1$ and earliest certification dates, the MILP certifies
each milestone exactly when its window opens, spending just enough
just in time to meet the progress threshold at each $e_{i,j}$.

\paragraph{Parameters.}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
$n,\,s_1,\,f_1,\,D^{\mathrm{plan}}$ & $1,\;1,\;6,\;6$\\
$\BAC_1,\;\pi_1,\;CP_1$ & $60,\;0.50,\;90$\\
$M_1,\;\theta,\;\phi$ & $3,\;(1/3,2/3,1),\;(1/3,1/3,1/3)$\\
$\bar\eta,\;e_{1,1},\;e_{1,2},\;e_{1,3}$ & $1.0,\;1,\;3,\;5$\\
$\alpha_1,\;\rho_1,\;\Omega_1,\;\mu_1,\;\tau_1^{\mathrm{tol}}$ &
  $0,\;0,\;2,\;0.50,\;2$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Milestone payments.} $R_{1,j}^{\mathrm{net}}=30$ each.

\paragraph{Optimal allocation.} Spend 20 at each $e_{1,j}$.
\[
  Z^* = \gamma^0\times10 + \gamma^2\times10 + \gamma^4\times10
      = 10 + 9.025 + 8.145 = \mathbf{27.17}.
\]

\paragraph{Assertions for pytest.}
\begin{enumerate}[label=A\arabic*.]
  \item $x_{1,1}=20\pm0.5$, $x_{1,3}=20\pm0.5$, $x_{1,5}=20\pm0.5$;
    zero otherwise.
  \item MS$j$ certified at $t\in\{1,3,5\}$ respectively.
  \item $|Z^*-27.17|\leq1.0$.

\section{Group 2: Discounting and Timing Incentives}
% ============================================================

$e_{i,j}$ is strictly better than front-loading. When a project can
be kept alive by partial spend that freezes the termination counter,
survival is optimal if and only if the discounted next milestone inflow
exceeds the discounted cost of survival allocations.
